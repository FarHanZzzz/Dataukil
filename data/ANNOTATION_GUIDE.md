# Three-label annotation guide

Judge only the supplied passage, including negation, qualification and scope. SUPPORTED means the text explicitly says or entails the claim. CONTRADICTED means it explicitly negates or supplies incompatible information. INSUFFICIENT means missing, unknown, pending/requested, ambiguous, or unrelated. An unverified author can textually support a statement; source authenticity is a separate field.

Examples: 'Cash received' supports cash received. 'Cash was not received' contradicts it. 'Cash may have been paid' is insufficient. 'Refund requested' is insufficient for 'refund completed'. 'QR completed' is insufficient for 'cash received'. 'These payments belong to different purchases' contradicts same-purchase linkage. 'Not merely requested; completed' supports completion despite containing a negation token.

Review all three languages against visible text, independently of the generator. Preserve source words. Record reviewer IDs, disagreements, label rationale and adjudication. All current labels are agent-authored candidates; independent human inspection has NOT occurred. Do not mark generated translations as independent customer incidents.
